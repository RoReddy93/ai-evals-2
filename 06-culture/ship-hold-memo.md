## The Answer

## The Arguments

### 1. Confirmed stale pricing

Evidence: Ascend IQ quotes $49 when the correct price is $59. This contradicts our promise of verified intelligence. With $50K+ enterprise renewals upcoming, inaccurate client-facing information creates a trust risk, although we have not quantified its effect on renewals.

### 2. Additional reliability concerns

Evidence: We have observed ~8/20 potentially fabricated answers which can undermine the trust in the product. They need to be fixed to ensure the product provides reliable and factual answers.

### 3. Tone requires assessment

Evidence: Unprofessional tone has been reported, but its severity has not been measured yet. Cosmetic wording should not independently block release; language that misrepresents certainty or removes important caveats should.
